Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, Answered Clearly.

How much does Safo Security cost?

Our services start at $500 for a Security Snapshot. Security Improvements start at $1,000, Business Security Assessments at $1,500, and Managed Security at $3,000 per month.

Do I need cybersecurity if I already have IT support?

Your IT provider may already cover some or all cybersecurity needs. We can help identify meaningful gaps in your existing coverage.

Can you guarantee we won't get hacked?

No provider can guarantee complete protection. Our goal is to reduce risks and improve security and recovery readiness.

Will you replace our existing IT company?

Not necessarily. We can coordinate with existing providers when responsibilities are clearly defined.

What businesses do you work with?

We focus on small and growing businesses, including clinics, accounting firms, legal practices, and professional services.

What happens during the free consultation?

We discuss your business, current systems, security concerns, and whether our services could be appropriate.

How long does implementation take?

Timelines depend on the size of your business and the agreed project scope.

Do you provide ongoing cybersecurity management?

Our managed security offering starts at $3,000 per month, with specific responsibilities defined in the agreement.

Do you offer 24/7 protection?

Monitoring hours, alert handling, and response commitments depend on the agreed scope. Around-the-clock response is not included unless expressly contracted and supported.

Are software licenses included?

Licensing and third-party costs are specified in each proposal.

Is there a long-term contract?

Contract length and cancellation terms are provided before any agreement is signed.
