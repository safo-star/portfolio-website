Before You Reach Out

Answers to what most owners ask first.

What does this actually cost?+

It depends on scope. A one-time Risk Assessment or Hardening project is quoted after a short discovery call, based on the systems and complexity involved. Ongoing Growth & Security Retainers — implementation plus continuous monitoring — typically run CA$1,200–$2,500/month. You always see the scope and investment before any paid work begins.

Will this disrupt our day-to-day operations?+

Standard reviews are passive and public-facing — they don't touch or interrupt live systems. Anything beyond that (active testing, configuration changes) only happens under a written scope you've approved in advance, usually scheduled around your team's availability.

How long does an engagement take?+

A Risk Assessment report usually comes back within a few business days of discovery. Hardening and implementation timelines depend on scope and are set together during the scoping conversation. Monitoring is ongoing once it starts.

We're not very technical — will we understand the report?+

Yes — that's the point. Findings are translated into business impact and priority, not just technical jargon, so you can make a confident decision without needing a security background.

Do you sign NDAs or handle sensitive data carefully?+

Yes. Scope, confidentiality and data handling are agreed in writing before any assessment begins, and standard reviews never go beyond authorized, passive, public-facing checks without your explicit approval.
